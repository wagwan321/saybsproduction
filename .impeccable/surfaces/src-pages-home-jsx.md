# Surface: SAYBS site (Home first, then Weddings, Wedding story, Films, About, Packages, Inquire)

Mode: Experience (the work leads). Inquire is Operate inside the same world.
Audience: engaged couples arriving from Instagram on phones, choosing a photographer and filmmaker, often a year ahead.
Job: feel the work, then check a date by WhatsApp or email.
Proof on hand: real weddings (Elissa & Roy, Valeria & Jacob, brides), real reels, three real testimonials, real vendor credits from the couples' posts. No prices.
Constraints: black and white only; each photo once across the site; no scroll effects that scale, shift or crop pictures; no em dashes; phones get a bottom tab bar, no sideways carousels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every wedding hangs as its own exhibition and the site is the gallery you walk through. Refuses the category default of a full-screen slideshow with a centred logo and a masonry grid.

OWN-WORLD: White gallery wall (#FFFFFF) and black ink (#0B0B0B), nothing between in the interface; the greys live only in the photographs. Prints sit in wide white mats with one hairline frame and a soft cast shadow, hung on a shared eye line. Small museum wall labels (Jost capitals, tracked) carry title, place, date. Exhibition titles are wall vinyl in Bodoni Moda with the logo's wide tracking. Films live in a black screening room. One ink weight: every rule and frame is the same 1px line. Raises: one governing proportion (the mat) sets every margin and gap; light travels with you (the room dims around an opened print); each wedding closes with a full credits block; two faces only, hierarchy from case, size, spacing.

STORY: The visitor sees a real SAYBS wedding at gallery scale, understands they shoot both photographs and films, walks into an exhibition, watches a film in the dark room, reads couples' words, and checks their date on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half: SAYBS as wall vinyl at exhibition-title scale, PHOTOGRAPHY & FILM between hairlines, "Timeless imagery. Films that feel like you." in italic Bodoni below. Right half: the lead print (Elissa & Roy) matted and hung, a second print cut by the right edge so the wall continues, and a wall label under the lead print ending in "See the wedding". Nav top right; "Check your date" is the last nav item, underlined. On phones: vinyl title, then the lead print full width with its label; bottom tab bar holds Check your date.

FORM: The Exhibition (black and white photography exhibition: wall, mats, wall labels, screening room), candidate 6 of 7 on my ordered list, assigned by the roll and approved by the user from two rendered sketches. seed:8d46d1eb5e70daf0. Signature interaction: Lights down. Opening a print dims the room to black and shows the print large with its label; arrows walk the wall, Esc brings the lights up. Motion grammar: lights (opacity and darkness), never moving or zooming the photographs on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
